      From first tap to front door

        Tell us what you need

        Your postcode, the help you want and when you want it. It will take about a minute, whether you're booking for yourself or for someone you love.

        Choose your carer

        See who is free near you, with reviews from other families, the checks they've passed and their hourly rate. Book the same carer again whenever you like.

      Safety

      Every carer checked before they can be booked

      Each carer will have a passport you can read before you book. If a check runs out, they come off the app until it's renewed.

      • Enhanced DBS checkThe highest level of criminal record check for working with adults.
      • Interview and referencesAn interview with our team and two references, one from a care role.
      • Training on recordThe Care Certificate or equivalent, plus any specialist courses, shown on their profile.
      • Reviews after every visitFamilies rate each visit, so standards stay high.
      Where we are with the CQC A2Z Cares isn't registered with the Care Quality Commission yet. We won't provide any care until we are.

      Questions

      Before we launch

      When are you launching?

      We're getting ready now. Register your interest and you'll be among the first to hear our launch date and which towns we're starting in.

      Are you registered with the CQC?

      Not yet. Home care in England has to be registered with the Care Quality Commission, and we won't provide any care until we are. Until then, this site is here so families and carers can register their interest.

      Which areas will you cover?

      We're starting in North Yorkshire, including York, Harrogate, Scarborough and the towns and villages in between. Where we go first depends on where people need us, so please tell us your town when you register.

      How much will it cost?

      You'll always see the full price before you book, with no contract and no joining fee. We'll publish our prices before we launch.

      Can I book for my mum or dad if I live far away?

      Yes. That's one of the main reasons we're building A2Z Cares. You'll be able to book from anywhere and add family members so they can see visit notes too.
